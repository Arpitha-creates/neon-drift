# Neon Drift — a synthwave arcade shooter

A neon space shooter drawn on HTML5 canvas. You auto-fire; your job is to drift, dodge and line up your shots as the waves get meaner.

## What it does
- Endless waves that ramp up in speed and volume
- Three enemy types — grunts, tanks and divers — each with their own movement and fire patterns
- Power-up drops: **Spread** (triple shot), **Shield**, and **Rapid** fire
- Particle explosions, screen shake, a parallax starfield and a day-glow palette
- Score, lives, wave counter and a persistent best score

## Controls
- **Move** — arrow keys or `A` / `D`, or drag on a touch screen
- **Fire** — automatic
- **Pause** — `P`

## Run it
Download `index.html` and open it in any modern browser, or enable GitHub Pages. No build step, no dependencies.

## Your data
Your best score is kept in local storage on your device.
